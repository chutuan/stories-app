## Mô tả đầy đủ (tối đa 4000 ký tự)

```text
Short serialised stories you can finish in one sitting: people who are underestimated, betrayed or written off, and what happens when the truth finally comes out.

WHAT YOU GET

• Original serials across Revenge, Family Drama, Secret Identity, Second Chance, CEO, Romance, Suspense and Mystery
• A reader built for long sessions: adjust font size, line spacing and brightness, and choose between four page themes including a night theme
• Narrated audio on selected stories: listen instead of read
• Some stories are free from the first chapter to the last
• Rate every chapter with one tap and see how other readers liked it
• No account, no sign-up, no email: open the app and start reading

HOW COINS WORK

Every new install starts with 90 coins, enough to unlock three chapters straight away. The first chapter of every story is free. Later chapters cost 30 coins each and stay unlocked on your device.

Coins are earned inside the app and are never sold for money. Reach a reading-time milestone, or watch an optional rewarded video for 60 coins, as often as you like. Videos depend on ad availability: if none is available while you are short of coins on a locked chapter, the app may add just enough to unlock it, a limited number of times a day.

PRIVACY

Stories has no accounts and ads are non-personalised. Your coins, unlocked chapters, saved stories, reading history and reading settings stay on your device. If you turn on the optional daily new-story notification, the app sends our server a notification token and your time zone so the notification arrives in your evening; turning it off deletes them. If you rate a chapter, the app sends the score with a random identifier that is not linked to you.

Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and a contact page are linked at the bottom of the Library and Rewards tabs, and at tunastory.com.
```
