4. Notifications: when you leave a chapter, or when you reopen the app later, a sheet "Never miss a new story" may appear. The sheet is shown at most 3 times, at least 3 days apart, and never once notifications are on. If notifications were declined in the system dialog, the sheet offers "Open Settings" instead. The setting can be switched on or off at any time in the Library tab under Notifications. At most one notification a day, in the evening.
